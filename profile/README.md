# civfix

### Fix your block. Together.

civfix is a free app that helps neighbors report problems on their street, see what's getting fixed, and team up to clean things up. Think trash piles, graffiti, broken sidewalks, that mattress that's been sitting on the corner for three weeks. You snap a photo, drop a pin, and it lands on a live map your whole neighborhood can see.

We're built by [Reach Out Los Angeles](https://reachoutla.org), a 501(c)(3) non-profit.

**[Try it at civfix.org](https://civfix.org)** (our mobile app is better though!!)

---

## How it works

- **See it. Snap it. Pin it.**
Take a photo or a short video, pick what it is, and drop it on the map. Three taps and you're done. You don't even need an account to send a report.

- **Your block, live.**
Every pin on the map is a report from someone nearby. Colors show the type of issue. Tap one to see photos, updates, and what people are saying about it. Anyone can browse, no sign up needed.

- **Show up. Talk it through.**
Find cleanups near you, RSVP, grab a shift, and log your volunteer hours. Chat with neighbors about a report or with your crew before the big day.

- **Track what gets fixed.**
Follow a report from the moment it's sent all the way to resolved, so you're never left wondering if anyone saw it.

## Some other cool stuff we have

- **Events.** Host a cleanup or other volunteer event, manage check-ins, and send quick updates to everyone coming.
- **Volunteer hours.** Build up a service record you can actually point to.
- **Organizations.** Nonprofits, community groups, and local government teams can get verified, and nonprofits can take donations right in the app.
- **Messages.** DMs, group chats, and chats tied to a specific report or crew.
- **Your language.** English, Spanish, Korean, and German. More to come.

## Privacy

We take this one seriously.

- No ads. No analytics. No trackers.
- GPS and camera info gets stripped from your photos before anything is saved.
- No passwords. Sign in with Apple, Google, or a code sent to your email.
- We don't sell your data. Ever. We run purely off donations & help.
- Location is only used while you're using the app, and you can still report without it.

Read the full details at [civfix.org/legal/privacy](https://civfix.org/legal/privacy).
